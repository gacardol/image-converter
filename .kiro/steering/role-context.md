# Account Manager (AM) — Role Context

## Role Overview
You are an Account Manager on the SSR-M Launch Focus team at Amazon Brazil, responsible for onboarding and activating ~400 new sellers per year on Amazon.com.br. Your focus is São Paulo state and Meli Badge sellers. You drive the full seller lifecycle from initial outreach through product launch and growth activation.

## Core Responsibilities
- **Mass seller outreach**: Email and WhatsApp campaigns to 50-100 sellers per week
- **Onboarding**: Conduct calls to guide sellers through first product listing and launch
- **Growth activation**: Enable sellers with Ads, Coupons, FBA, Brand Registry, and Automated Pricing
- **Pipeline management**: Track all opportunities in Astro (BD → Evaluation → Closed Won)
- **Performance monitoring**: Use QuickSight dashboards (Sales Hub 1.0/2.0, Scorecard) to track seller health
- **Reporting**: Monthly performance reports for leadership with metrics and trends
- **Cross-org coordination**: Work with FBA, listing, and integration teams to unblock sellers

## How Your Assistant Can Help
- **Email drafting**: Generate Portuguese outreach templates, follow-up emails, and activation instructions
- **Meeting prep**: Pull seller context before onboarding calls — pipeline stage, listing status, growth lever readiness
- **Pipeline reviews**: Summarize Astro pipeline, flag stalled opportunities, identify next actions
- **Data analysis**: Help interpret QuickSight dashboard data, spot trends, build reports
- **Report generation**: Draft monthly leadership reports with highlights, lowlights, and action items
- **Template management**: Maintain and improve email/WhatsApp templates for different seller segments
- **Cross-org communication**: Draft clear asks to FBA, listing, and integration teams

## Role-Specific Workflows

### Weekly Seller Outreach Cycle
1. Monday: Pull new seller list from pipeline, segment by category/size
2. Tuesday-Wednesday: Send batch outreach emails (personalized templates)
3. Thursday: Follow up on non-responders via WhatsApp
4. Friday: Update pipeline with responses, schedule onboarding calls for next week

### Seller Onboarding Flow
1. Pre-call: Review seller profile, prepare talking points
2. Call: Walk through first product listing, explain growth levers
3. Post-call: Send follow-up email with action items, update Astro
4. Follow-up (1 week): Check if seller listed first product, offer help

### Monthly Reporting Cycle
1. Week 3: Start pulling dashboard data for the month
2. Week 4: Compile metrics, identify trends and outliers
3. Month end: Draft report, get peer review, submit to leadership

## Key Tools for Your Role
- **Astro**: Your pipeline source of truth — every seller interaction logged here
- **QuickSight**: Performance dashboards — Sales Hub for sales data, Scorecard for KPIs
- **Outlook + MCP**: High-volume email with AI assistance for drafting and sending
- **Gandalf + MCP**: Excel automation for data analysis and report formatting
- **AWSentral + MCP**: Salesforce/CRM data for account and opportunity details

## Seller Segments You Manage
- **São Paulo state sellers**: Geographic focus, various categories
- **Meli Badge sellers**: Sellers migrating from or also selling on Mercado Livre
- **New launches**: Sellers in their first 90 days on Amazon.com.br

## Growth Levers (activation order)
1. **First product listing** — the critical first step
2. **Automated Pricing** — easy win, helps competitiveness
3. **Ads (Sponsored Products)** — drives visibility
4. **Coupons** — boosts conversion
5. **FBA** — improves delivery and Buy Box eligibility
6. **Brand Registry** — for brand owners, unlocks A+ Content and brand protection
