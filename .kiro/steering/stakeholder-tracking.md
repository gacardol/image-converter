# Stakeholder Tracking

## Why This Matters
As an AM on SSR-M Launch Focus, you don't work in isolation. Unblocking sellers often means working across teams — FBA, category, listing quality, integration, ads. Knowing who to contact and how to escalate saves days of back-and-forth.

## Escalation Paths

### Level 1 — Self-Service (Try First)
- Submit SIM ticket to the relevant team
- Post in team Slack channel with clear ask
- Check wiki for known solutions or workarounds

### Level 2 — Direct Contact
- Ping the team's POC directly on Slack
- Reference the SIM ticket number
- Include: seller name, issue, business impact, what you've tried

### Level 3 — Manager Escalation
- Loop in your manager when:
  - SLA has been exceeded with no response
  - Multiple sellers affected by the same issue
  - Revenue impact is significant
  - You've tried L1 and L2 with no resolution
- Use the escalation template from `#leadership-status-updates`

### Level 4 — Leadership Escalation
- Your manager escalates to their counterpart on the blocking team
- Only for critical/systemic issues affecting multiple sellers or targets

## Cross-Org Communication Best Practices

### When Requesting Help
Always include in your first message:
1. **Seller identifier** — store name or merchant ID (not personal data)
2. **Issue** — one sentence, what's wrong
3. **Impact** — how many sellers, revenue at risk, timeline
4. **What you've tried** — so they don't repeat steps
5. **Ask** — exactly what you need from them

### Template: Cross-Org Ask (Slack/SIM)

```
Hi [TEAM/NAME] 👋

Requesting help with: [ONE-LINE ISSUE]

**Seller**: [Store name / Merchant ID]
**Issue**: [What's happening — 1-2 sentences]
**Impact**: [X sellers affected / R$X at risk / seller at risk of churning]
**What I've tried**: [Steps taken so far]
**Ask**: [Specific action needed]
**SIM ticket**: [TICKET-ID if exists]

Timeline: [When this needs resolution — be realistic]

Thanks!
```

### Template: Follow-Up (No Response After SLA)

```
Hi [TEAM/NAME],

Following up on [ISSUE] from [DATE] (SIM: [TICKET-ID]).

Seller [STORE NAME] is still blocked on [ISSUE]. It's been [X] days past the expected SLA.

Impact has grown: [Updated impact — e.g., seller threatening to leave, more sellers affected].

Can someone take a look today? Happy to jump on a quick call if that helps.

Thanks!
```

## Relationship Health Indicators

Track how well your cross-org relationships are working:

### 🟢 Healthy
- Responses within SLA
- POC knows you by name
- Proactive heads-up on issues affecting your sellers
- Willing to prioritize urgent requests

### 🟡 Needs Investment
- Responses at or slightly past SLA
- Have to re-explain your role each time
- No proactive communication
- Requests handled but not prioritized

### 🔴 Needs Repair
- Consistently past SLA or no response
- Tickets closed without resolution
- Adversarial or dismissive tone
- Your sellers are suffering because of this gap

### Actions to Improve Relationships
- **Coffee chat**: 15-min intro call with new POCs — builds goodwill
- **Thank publicly**: When a team unblocks your seller quickly, thank them in their Slack channel
- **Share wins**: When a seller succeeds because of their help, let them know
- **Batch requests**: Don't ping 5 times a day — batch your asks when possible
- **Be specific**: Vague asks get vague responses. Specific asks get action.

## Seasonal / Cyclical Awareness

| Period | What Happens | Stakeholder Impact |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Black Friday prep (Sep-Oct)** | FBA capacity tightens, category team busy | Plan FBA shipments early, expect slower approvals |
| **Prime Day prep** | Ads team overloaded, listing quality stricter | Submit ad campaigns early, double-check listings |
| **Year-end (Dec)** | Slower responses, PTO season | Front-load escalations, set seller expectations |
| **Q1 (Jan-Feb)** | New targets, team reorgs possible | Re-confirm POCs, update contact list |
| **Mid-year review** | Leadership focused on metrics | Good time for escalations with data backing |
