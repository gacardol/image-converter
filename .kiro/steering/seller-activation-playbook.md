# Seller Activation Playbook

## Growth Lever Activation Order
Activate in this sequence for maximum impact. Each lever builds on the previous one.

### 1. First Product Listing (Day 1-7)
**Readiness**: Seller has completed registration
**Goal**: Get first ASIN live on Amazon.com.br
**Checklist**:
- [ ] Seller has active Seller Central account
- [ ] Product info ready (title, description, images, price)
- [ ] Category selected and approved (if gated)
- [ ] First listing published and buyable
**Success metric**: First product live and buyable
**Common blockers**: Category approval, image requirements, pricing errors

### 2. Automated Pricing (Day 7-14)
**Readiness**: At least 1 active listing
**Goal**: Enable competitive pricing without manual monitoring
**Checklist**:
- [ ] Seller has active listings with prices set
- [ ] Automated Pricing rule created (match lowest or stay competitive)
- [ ] Min/max price boundaries configured
**Success metric**: Automated Pricing rule active on 80%+ of listings
**Common blockers**: Seller worried about margin erosion — explain min price protection

### 3. Sponsored Products / Ads (Day 14-30)
**Readiness**: Active listings with good content (title, images, description)
**Goal**: Drive visibility and first sales through advertising
**Checklist**:
- [ ] At least 3-5 active listings
- [ ] Product detail pages optimized (good titles, 5+ images, bullet points)
- [ ] First automatic campaign created
- [ ] Daily budget set (suggest R$20-50/day to start)
**Success metric**: First ad campaign running with impressions
**Common blockers**: Low budget concerns — explain ACOS and ROI

### 4. Coupons (Day 30-45)
**Readiness**: Active listings with some sales history
**Goal**: Boost conversion rate on key products
**Checklist**:
- [ ] Products with at least some sales/reviews
- [ ] Coupon created (5-15% off recommended)
- [ ] Budget allocated for coupon redemptions
**Success metric**: Coupon active and being redeemed
**Common blockers**: Margin concerns — show conversion lift data

### 5. FBA — Fulfillment by Amazon (Day 30-60)
**Readiness**: Consistent sales, ready to scale
**Goal**: Improve delivery speed and Buy Box eligibility
**Checklist**:
- [ ] Seller understands FBA fees and process
- [ ] Top-selling products identified for FBA
- [ ] First shipment plan created
- [ ] Products shipped to fulfillment center
**Success metric**: First FBA order fulfilled
**Common blockers**: Fee concerns, inventory management, shipping logistics

### 6. Brand Registry (When applicable)
**Readiness**: Seller owns a registered trademark (INPI)
**Goal**: Unlock brand protection and A+ Content
**Checklist**:
- [ ] Trademark registered with INPI (or pending with serial number)
- [ ] Brand Registry application submitted
- [ ] Brand approved in Brand Registry
- [ ] A+ Content created for top products
**Success metric**: Brand Registry approved, A+ Content live
**Common blockers**: No trademark, trademark in wrong name, INPI delays

## Seller Health Indicators

### Green (Healthy) 🟢
- Active listings growing
- Orders in last 7 days
- At least 2 growth levers active
- Responding to communications

### Yellow (Needs Attention) 🟡
- No new listings in 2+ weeks
- Orders declining week-over-week
- Only 1 growth lever active
- Slow to respond (3+ days)

### Red (At Risk) 🔴
- No orders in 14+ days
- No response to outreach in 2+ weeks
- Account health issues (ODR, late shipment)
- Seller expressing intent to leave platform

## Meli Badge Seller — Special Considerations
- These sellers already know e-commerce — don't over-explain basics
- Focus on Amazon-specific advantages: FBA, Brand Registry, Ads
- Acknowledge their Mercado Livre experience as a strength
- Common concern: "Why should I sell on Amazon too?" — emphasize incremental customer base
- They often have inventory and logistics already set up — fast-track to FBA

## Seller Objection Handling

| Objection | Response Approach |
|-----------|-------------------|
| "Fees are too high" | Compare total cost including logistics; show FBA value |
| "I don't have time" | Emphasize Automated Pricing and FBA as time-savers |
| "Not enough traffic" | Show Ads ROI data; Amazon customer base is growing in BR |
| "I'm doing well on Mercado Livre" | Position as incremental channel, not replacement |
| "Too complicated" | Offer hands-on onboarding call; break into small steps |
