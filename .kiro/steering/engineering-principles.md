# Engineering Principles

## Data Accuracy
- Double-check numbers before including in reports or emails
- When pulling from dashboards, note the date/time of the data
- Flag when data seems inconsistent or outdated
- Always specify the source of metrics (which dashboard, which date range)

## Communication Quality
- Seller-facing emails: professional but warm, in Brazilian Portuguese
- Internal communications: clear, concise, data-backed
- Always proofread for seller names, numbers, and dates before sending
- Use consistent formatting in templates for brand professionalism

## Pipeline Hygiene
- Every seller interaction should be reflected in Astro
- Opportunities should have current stage, notes, and next action
- Stalled opportunities (no movement in 2+ weeks) should be flagged
- Closed Won should only be used when seller has actually launched

## Reporting Standards
- Reports should tell a story: what happened, why, what's next
- Include both absolute numbers and trends (week-over-week, month-over-month)
- Highlight top performers and at-risk sellers
- Always include action items — reports without next steps are incomplete

## Security & Privacy
- Never share seller PII (personal data) in Slack channels
- Use seller store names or IDs in internal communications when possible
- Be careful with LGPD (Brazilian data protection law) compliance
- Don't store seller personal contact info in unsecured locations
